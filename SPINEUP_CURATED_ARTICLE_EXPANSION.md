# SpineUp Curated Article Expansion

## Purpose

This expansion increases the number of native SpineUp reading briefs without presenting arbitrary RSS pages as if they were reviewed app content. Each brief is a concise paraphrase of a named source, links to the original page, includes a safety label, and states its limitations.

## New briefs

| Mindfulness for everyday moments | [NHS Mindfulness](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/tips-and-support/mindfulness/) | Mindfulness and stress | Native curated brief | Explains present-moment awareness and gentle practice; does not claim mindfulness is suitable for everyone or replace mental-health care. |

## Content policy applied

The new items are intentionally short and written in SpineUp’s non-diagnostic voice. The app records and educates; it does not identify conditions, predict progression, prescribe treatment, or determine whether an activity is suitable for a particular person. Every item links to its original source page so users can read the complete, current guidance.

The mindfulness and stress items are separated from scoliosis education so users can find wellbeing support without being told that stress or mindfulness explains their physical symptoms. The sleep and movement items are framed as general self-management topics, not as guaranteed solutions for pain, scoliosis, anxiety, or sleep problems.
